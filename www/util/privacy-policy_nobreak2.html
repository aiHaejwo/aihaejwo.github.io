Privacy Policy

Application: AeroBrakes Developer: Ttalkkag Effective Date: September 28, 2026
Summary: AeroBrakes is designed with user privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or exact physical locations. Game progress is saved strictly on your local device. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may process non-sensitive device identifiers to serve advertisements in compliance with privacy regulations.

1. Information Collection and Use

AeroBrakes is an offline-capable arcade flight game. We do not require account registration, login, or personal profile creation. We do not host central user databases.

  • Local Game Data: Your high scores, flight distance records, collected energy cores, unlocked aircraft, and pilot settings are stored exclusively in your device's private application storage. This data never leaves your device unless you delete the app or clear app data.
  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect or request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

3. Device Permissions

AeroBrakes only requests the minimum necessary permissions required for core game functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load advertising assets from Google AdMob and verify consent forms via the Google User Messaging Platform. The game remains fully playable offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required to provide haptic feedback effects during near-miss supersonic grazes and crashes. Haptic vibration can be disabled at any time in the in-game Pilot Settings menu.

4. User Rights & Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

Users have full control over advertising personalization and diagnostic data:

  • Android Device Settings: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Consent Settings (EEA / UK): Users located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom are presented with a consent dialog upon first launch and can update their consent preferences at any time.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

AeroBrakes is rated for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Third-party advertising partners are configured to comply with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable child privacy regulations.

6. Security of Your Information

We value your trust. Because all save data is stored locally within sandboxed app storage and all network communications by Google SDKs utilize encrypted TLS/HTTPS protocols, your gameplay data remains safe and protected from unauthorized access.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates in game features, legal regulations, or Google Play Developer Policies. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
