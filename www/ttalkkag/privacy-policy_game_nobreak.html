Privacy Policy for NoBrakes

App Name: NoBrakes | Package: com.ttalkkag.nobrakes | Effective Date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoBrakes ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data collected or processed through the App.

1. Information We Collect

NoBrakes does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, phone number, or email address directly on our own servers.

Locally Stored Data:

  • Game Progress & Settings: Your high scores, accumulated coins, unlocked cars, current level, sound/vibration toggles, and steering sensitivity are saved locally on your device storage (save.dat). This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Automatically Collected Data by Third-Party Services:

The App integrates third-party advertising services (Google AdMob). These services may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with their respective privacy policies, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention)
  • Device type, operating system version, and performance metrics
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completions)

2. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party SDKs to deliver advertisements and analyze ad performance. Please refer to their privacy policies for details on how they collect and handle data:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to provide European Economic Area (EEA) and UK users with GDPR-compliant consent management forms.

3. Device Permissions Used

The App requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for game functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch and display ads from Google AdMob and download consent forms via UMP.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required to provide haptic vibration feedback for in-game collisions and near-miss effects.

4. User Choices & Consent Management (GDPR / CCPA)

EEA & UK Users: If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are presented with a consent dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) upon initial startup. You can customize or withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

NoBrakes is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to any third party through our App, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates in game features or legal regulations. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Effective Date".
